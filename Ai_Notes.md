# AI Notes

## What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence by machines. It enables computers to perform tasks such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, and decision-making.

## What is Machine Learning (ML)?
Machine Learning is a branch of AI that allows computers to learn from data and improve their performance without being explicitly programmed.

## What is Deep Learning?
Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers to analyze data and solve complex problems.

## Difference between AI, ML, and Deep Learning

- AI: Makes machines intelligent.
- ML: Enables machines to learn from data.
- Deep Learning: Uses neural networks for advanced learning.

## Real-World Applications of AI

1. Virtual Assistants (Siri, Alexa, Google Assistant)
2. Self-driving Cars
3. Healthcare Diagnosis
4. Face Recognition
5. Recommendation Systems (Netflix, YouTube, Amazon)
